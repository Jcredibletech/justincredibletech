Tesla's biggest self-driving software update in months is reaching its first owners, and the screen is what everyone is talking about.

## What happened

On **Saturday, November 21, 2026**, Tesla announced on X that **FSD (Supervised) v15** is "now starting to roll out." Tracking site [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/software-updates/version/2026.38.6.11/release-notes) lists it as software 2026.38.6.11 (FSD v15.0.1), going first to **Early Access owners with HW4 (AI4) computers**. Elon Musk has said a wide release will come with Tesla's December holiday update.

Tesla's release notes, as published by [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/11/21/tesla-fsd-v15-rollout-25x-safety-reality-view/) and Not a Tesla App, include:

- An upgraded vision system shared between FSD, Robotaxi and the Optimus robot, which Tesla says improves the **critical intervention rate by 25x**.
- **Tesla Reality View**, a real-time generated visualization from a virtual camera above the car, which Tesla says lets you "see & control what FSD is planning to do."
- Better detection of **road signs, potholes, debris and small animals**, better avoidance of **deep standing water**, and better speed handling in **school zones** and bad weather.
- Driver monitoring that works more often with **hats and sunglasses**, and is less strict in higher-speed driver profiles.
- **Speed-camera slowdowns** (which drivers can cancel) and a **Bike Rack Mode** that allows FSD with a hitch accessory.

## Why people are talking about it

Reality View replaces the old gray-shapes display with a photo-like rendering of the street. "It's like GTA," Musk posted, according to [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/its-like-gta-musk-touts-video-game-like-update-to-tesla-fsd-2000843584). Tesla's own images are promotional, so what owners see may differ.

The bigger story may be the target list. Electrek's Fred Lambert wrote that the improvements read "like a list of FSD's biggest current weaknesses," pointing to a September test by Brussels nonprofit Johanna.be in which FSD exceeded the limit in 55% of the 30 km/h zones it drove through.

## What to keep in mind

- The **25x** figure is Tesla's own claim; it hasn't published the underlying data.
- FSD is still **"Supervised"**: the driver must pay attention and be ready to take over.
- Lambert also cautioned that "FSD has never improved in a straight line," noting point releases sometimes go backward. Owner reports over the next few weeks will be the real test.
- Cars with older **HW3** computers are not part of this rollout.
