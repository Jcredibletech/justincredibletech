If you drive a hybrid, you've got a little cousin of the tech that just won Formula 1's team title. Mercedes locked up the **2026 Constructors' Championship** at Interlagos today, in the first season of F1's new rules, where about half of a car's power comes from electricity.

## What happened

- **Lando Norris won the São Paulo Grand Prix** for McLaren from pole, in a race that started wet, dried out, then got hit by a late shower. He beat Max Verstappen by 14.6 seconds ([Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/norris-beats-verstappen-in-wet-sao-paulo-gp-thriller-as-mercedes-win-constructors-championship.7JN7MXLZf5lk5l0C3gfKGI), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/f1-brazil-sao-paulo-norris-antonelli-844f985f84dd19c23947ddcad31fbf76)).
- **Verstappen went from 21st on the grid to 2nd** after an engine penalty, with Red Bull teammate Isack Hadjar third ([BBC Sport](https://www.bbc.com/sport/formula1/articles/c35yn19r7zkvo)).
- **Mercedes still clinched the title.** George Russell and Kimi Antonelli both spun at points, but finished 4th and 5th. That gave Mercedes an unassailable lead over Ferrari with **three rounds to spare** ([Sky Sports](https://www.skysports.com/f1/news/13609694/sao-paulo-gp-lando-norris-wins-wet-race-for-mclaren-as-mercedes-seal-2026-f1-constructors-championship-in-brazil)). It's the team's ninth constructors' crown and its first since 2021, a week after Antonelli wrapped up the drivers' title (BBC Sport).

## Why the tech matters

2026 brought the biggest power unit shake-up in F1 history. Teams kept the 1.6-liter turbo V6 but dropped the complicated MGU-H, which recovered energy from exhaust heat. The MGU-K, the motor-generator that harvests energy under braking, nearly tripled in output, **from 120 kW to 350 kW**. That moves the split from about 20% electric to about **50/50** ([Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/2026-regulations-explained-all-you-need-to-know-about-f1-their-new-power-units.14jfv7a36905uDJDdNyfQd)).

The cars also now run on **100% advanced sustainable fuel**, made from carbon capture, municipal waste or non-food biomass ([FIA](https://www.fia.com/file/186874/download)). F1 says it dropped the MGU-H partly because road car makers never adopted it, while braking-energy recovery like the MGU-K is already in road cars. That's a big reason Audi, Honda and Ford, via Red Bull Powertrains, signed on as engine makers.

So Mercedes didn't just build the fastest car. It figured out the new hybrid formula first, and it did that with a gas engine still doing half the work.

## My take

This is the part I love. F1 didn't ban the combustion engine. It made the V6 smarter, paired it with a serious electric motor, and switched the fuel. The racing is still loud and fast, and the tech trickles down to the hybrids people actually buy. To me that's a better model than governments telling everyone to go full EV by a certain date. Let engineers compete and let buyers choose.
