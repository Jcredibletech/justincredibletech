If you checked your 401(k) after last week, today probably looked a lot better.

U.S. stocks had their best day since April 2025 on Monday, November 23, 2026, after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. According to [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/wall-street-futures-rebound-after-rough-week-for-ailinked-shares-5044594), the S&P 500 rose 326.78 points, or 4.73%, to 7,237.17. The Nasdaq composite rose 6.51% to 24,089.56, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 1,447.98 points, or 3.01%, to 49,595.31.

[MarketWatch](https://www.marketwatch.com/story/u-s-stocks-see-best-day-in-more-than-a-year-as-return-of-jensen-huang-revives-ai-enthusiasm-c3b9d274), citing Dow Jones Market Data, said it was the S&P 500's largest one-day percentage gain since April 9, 2025, the day President Trump paused most of his "Liberation Day" tariffs. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-november-23-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-surge-after-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-182039397.html) noted the S&P 500's gain came after seven straight days of declines.

## Nvidia led the way

Nvidia shares closed up 31.5%, their biggest one-day percentage gain since May 9, 2003, Reuters reported. Nvidia's board announced Monday morning that Huang was back as president, CEO and a board director ([our earlier story](https://justincredibletech.com/blog/update-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo/)). Huang had resigned earlier this month.

The rest of tech followed. Per Reuters and [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/anthropic-nvidia-shares.html):

- The S&P 500 technology sector jumped 9%, and the Philadelphia semiconductor index rose 14%, its best day since April 2025.
- Meta rose about 6.6%, Alphabet 6.3% and Amazon 3.5%.
- CoreWeave, a cloud company that rents out Nvidia-powered AI servers, jumped 22.3%.
- The Nasdaq-100 added more than $2 trillion in market value in one day.

One stock that didn't join in: Anthropic, which closed down about 1.4% in its third day of trading, CNBC reported.

## The catch

Monday's rally only won back part of the damage. Last week the Nasdaq fell 10%, its biggest weekly drop since the March 2020 pandemic sell-off, and the S&P 500 and Dow both closed Friday in correction territory. The [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/stocks-markets-rates-technology-ai-anthropic-49ba1beba78fa4ed2bd5898029f79c91) said stocks recovered just over half of last week's loss, which it called one of the worst weeks since the 2008 financial crisis.

## What's next

Huang is scheduled to testify publicly before the Senate Banking Committee on Thursday, December 3, at 10 a.m. ET, as the only witness at a hearing titled "Protecting America's AI Advantage: Oversight of U.S. Export Controls," [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6238064-nvidia-ceo-ai-hearing-december/) reported. It would be his first public hearing before Congress. Lawmakers from both parties criticized Nvidia's decision on Monday, Reuters reported.

This is news, not investment advice. A single day's move, up or down, doesn't say where markets go next.
