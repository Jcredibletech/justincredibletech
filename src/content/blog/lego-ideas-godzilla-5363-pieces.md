Five thousand, three hundred sixty-three pieces. That's what it takes to build the King of the Monsters out of bricks.

LEGO picked the perfect day to show it off. November 3 is Godzilla Day, the anniversary of the original 1954 film's release in Japan, and this morning the LEGO Group revealed **LEGO Ideas Godzilla (21375)**, the first official LEGO set featuring Godzilla. It's based on the Godzilla design from Legendary's MonsterVerse movies, not the classic Toho look.

## The numbers

Here are the official specs, from [LEGO's announcement on Godzilla.com](https://godzilla.com/blogs/news/godzilla-arrives-in-lego%c2%ae-brick-form) and [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/lego-godzilla-set-images-release-date-price/):

- **Pieces:** 5,363
- **Size:** about 27.5 inches (70 cm) tall when posed with the heat ray, 10.5 inches wide and 15.5 inches long
- **Price:** $449.99 / £349.99 / €399.99
- **Age:** 18+
- **On sale:** November 27, 2026, at LEGO Stores and LEGO.com

LEGO says a standard minifigure only comes up to the creature's ankle. Clear some shelf space.

## A fan built the first version

This one didn't start in LEGO's design studio. It came through **LEGO Ideas**, the program where fans pitch their own builds and the community votes them up. New York-based designer Matthew "MattE720" Esposito submitted the original model, and it took more than two years to reach the 10,000 supporters a project needs before LEGO's review board will consider it.

"The head was definitely the biggest challenge," Esposito said in LEGO's release. The finished set is now the **biggest LEGO Ideas set ever released**, per LEGO and [The Brick Fan](https://www.thebrickfan.com/lego-ideas-godzilla-21375-officially-announced/).

## The engineering is the fun part

Building a monster that tall out of plastic is a real structural problem. According to LEGO, the shoulders, elbows, wrists and fingers are all adjustable, and the head tilts. You can build the tail curving to the left or the right, and the red brick-built tongue swaps out for Godzilla's glowing blue heat ray.

[StoneWars' hands-on breakdown](https://stonewars.com/news/lego-21375-godzilla-revealed/) points out the trade-offs. The tail is built rigid on purpose so it works as a support, and the front limbs couldn't hold up more than 5,000 pieces, so don't expect a four-legged pose. StoneWars also notes it ties for the 15th-tallest LEGO set ever, alongside the Icons NASA Artemis Space Launch System.

There's a digital side too. LEGO says the set works with the **LEGO Builder app**, which offers 3D digital instructions you can rotate and zoom, plus progress tracking. That's a big help on a 5,000-plus piece build.

## Black Friday bonus

If you buy it between November 27 and November 30, LEGO is including a free gift with purchase: the 359-piece **Godzilla Logo and Footprint** set.

It's a big week for Godzilla fans in general. *Godzilla Minus Zero* hits theaters this week, the *Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee* remaster is out, and the MonsterVerse returns in 2027 with *Godzilla x Kong: Supernova*.
