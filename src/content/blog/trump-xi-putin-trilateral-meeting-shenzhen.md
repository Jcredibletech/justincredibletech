The leaders of the world's three largest nuclear powers are set to sit down together for the first time. White House spokeswoman Olivia Wales told reporters on Friday, November 13, that President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin will hold a **trilateral meeting** on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in **Shenzhen, China**, next week, according to the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ckz74jzpy18do) and [CNA](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/east-asia/trump-xi-putin-trilateral-summit-apec-6551001).

## The schedule

- **Monday:** Trump leaves Washington.
- **Tuesday:** He attends the ASEAN summit in the Philippines.
- **Wednesday night:** He arrives in Shenzhen for the two-day APEC summit.
- **Thursday, November 19:** A bilateral working lunch and meeting with Xi, their fourth meeting since Trump returned to office, then the three-way meeting with Xi and Putin. CNA reports the Kremlin confirmed the November 19 date on Saturday.

Trump has no separate one-on-one scheduled with Putin, CNA reports.

## What's on the agenda

**Nuclear weapons.** "The president has made it clear that, among other topics, he will discuss the reduction of nuclear weapons in the world, whose proliferation President Trump believes is very dangerous," Wales said. The BBC notes the last major U.S.-Russia nuclear arms treaty expired in February, and Trump has said he wants a better deal that includes China.

**Iran.** A senior U.S. official told reporters Trump "does intend to talk about Iran" in the meeting. China is Iran's largest oil buyer and trade partner.

**Not on the agenda:** Ukraine. The official said the U.S. has a separate negotiating track with Kyiv and Moscow, and "China is not really a part of that track." U.S. officials also said they do not expect the Nvidia scandal or chip export controls to come up. "We do not negotiate American export controls," one official said.

## What each side is saying

A senior U.S. official said no agreement is expected from the talks, according to the BBC, citing AP and Reuters. Earlier this month, Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said there were "signals confirming readiness" for such talks. Chinese state media reported Xi would hold "bilateral" talks at APEC but, as of Friday, had not reported on the three-way meeting.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has warned the meeting could become a Yalta-style "redistribution of spheres of influence," CNA reports, a reference to the 1945 summit of Allied leaders. Elon Musk will travel with Trump but will not take part in the meeting with Xi and Putin, U.S. officials said.

## Why it matters

No sitting U.S., Chinese and Russian leaders have met as a trio like this before. Even without a deal, the meeting could signal whether a three-way nuclear arms framework is possible, something analysts have said could take years.
