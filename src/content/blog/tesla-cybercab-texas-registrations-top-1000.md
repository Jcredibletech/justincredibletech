Tesla now has **more than 1,000 Cybercabs** registered in Texas. Texas Department of Motor Vehicles records listed **1,841** automated vehicles under Tesla Robotaxi LLC on **Wednesday, Nov. 26**: **1,107 Cybercabs** and **734 Model Ys**, according to [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/11/26/tesla-now-has-more-than-1000-cybercabs-on-texas-robotaxi-roster/) and [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/5095/teslas-cybercab-fleet-tops-1000-vehicles-in-texas).

## What happened

The latest batch added **285 Cybercabs** to Tesla's Texas roster since the previous count, which had 822. Not a Tesla App, citing DMV data shared by Sawyer Merritt, calls it the biggest single jump in Cybercab registrations so far. TeslaNorth reports the new batch is 2027-model-year Cybercabs, citing the FSD Database.

That shifts the mix. Cybercabs are now about **60%** of Tesla's Texas autonomous fleet, up from about 53% at the last check.

The Cybercab is Tesla's purpose-built two-seat robotaxi with **no steering wheel or pedals**. Tesla began paid Cybercab rides in Austin in September and [opened public rides in Dallas on Nov. 19](/blog/update-tesla-cybercab-public-rides-dallas/). Not a Tesla App reports Tesla has since nearly doubled the Dallas service area to about 150 square miles.

## The catch: registered isn't the same as on the road

Both outlets stress that the roster counts vehicles Tesla has **registered** under its Texas authorization. It doesn't show how many are giving rides at any moment, or which city gets them next. The last official number from Tesla came in October, when it said it had more than 300 Cybercabs running in Austin. Tesla's robotaxi mileage counter, which recently passed 6 million autonomous miles, is a separate measure.

## Why it matters

A fleet only matters if it scales, and this is the clearest public sign yet of how many purpose-built robotaxis Tesla is putting into service. It also raises the stakes for safety scrutiny. Competitors are under the microscope too: Waymo just [recalled 4,488 robotaxis after some entered closed freeway lanes](/blog/update-waymo-recall-4488-robotaxis-closed-freeway-lanes/).

## What's next

Not a Tesla App reports Tesla has started selling Cybercabs to select customers in Texas, with first deliveries expected next month. Elon Musk has said owners could start adding their cars to the Robotaxi network as early as December. Those are company timelines, and Tesla's dates have slipped before. Watch for whether new cities, like Houston, San Antonio or Fort Worth, show up next, and whether Tesla publishes an updated count of Cybercabs actually in service.
