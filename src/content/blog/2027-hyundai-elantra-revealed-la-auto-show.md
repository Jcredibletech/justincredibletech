Sedans aren't dead yet. Hyundai just gave one of America's most popular compact cars its biggest makeover in years.

## What happened

On **Thursday, November 19, 2026**, Hyundai unveiled the eighth-generation **2027 Elantra** and the **2028 Elantra Hybrid** at **AutoMobility LA**, the media day of the Los Angeles Auto Show, according to [Hyundai's announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/2027-hyundai-elantra-and-2028-elantra-hybrid-revealed-at-los-angeles-auto-show-302945048.html). [Road & Track](https://www.roadandtrack.com/news/a74470997/2027-hyundai-elantra-details/) and [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/cars/news/auto-shows/2026/11/19/2027-hyundai-elantra-la-auto-show-reveal/92864371007/) also covered the reveal. The show opens to the public today, **November 20**, and runs through **November 29**.

## What's new

- **Design:** The Elantra adopts Hyundai's **"Art of Steel"** look, first shown on the Boulder concept: a boxier three-box shape, wide fenders and sharp lighting.
- **Size:** Road & Track reports it's **3.3 inches longer**, **1.1 inches wider** and has a **1.1-inch longer wheelbase** than the outgoing car. Hyundai claims best-in-class rear legroom of **39.1 inches**.
- **Tech:** Every Elantra gets a **12.9-inch** screen running **Pleos Connect**, Hyundai's new Android Automotive-based system, with the Gleo AI voice assistant and over-the-air updates.
- **Engines:** a **2.0-liter four-cylinder** with **146 hp** and a CVT, or a **1.6-liter hybrid** with **154 hp** and a six-speed dual-clutch, per Road & Track.
- **Hybrid extras:** Hyundai is targeting **up to 53 mpg combined** for the hybrid, based on its own testing; EPA numbers are still pending. A new **"Stay Mode"** can run climate, audio and charging ports for roughly 20 to 30 minutes while parked with the engine off.

The gas car arrives as a 2027 model and the hybrid as a 2028 model. **Pricing and U.S. on-sale timing haven't been announced.**

## What Hyundai says

"For many customers, ELANTRA is their first experience with the Hyundai brand," said José Muñoz, president and CEO of Hyundai Motor Company. Hyundai says the Elantra is its all-time best-selling nameplate both in the U.S. and globally.

## Why it matters

As many brands drop small sedans, Hyundai is betting that a roomier, more stylish and more efficient Elantra can keep buyers who want an affordable new car. With average new-car prices high, where Hyundai sets the price will matter as much as the styling.
