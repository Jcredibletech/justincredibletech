Five days after he resigned, Jensen Huang raised his hand to come back. A few hours later, Nvidia's board answered on its own earnings call: not happening. This is the latest chapter in a story I've followed since the [board first weighed pushing him out](https://justincredibletech.com/blog/nvidia-board-weighs-ousting-jensen-huang/) and [his resignation on November 12](https://justincredibletech.com/blog/jensen-huang-resigns-nvidia-ceo/).

## What Huang said

In his first public statement since stepping down, which he posted on X on Tuesday, Huang struck a contrite tone ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-jensen-huang-statement.html), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwx2en2vrxmdo)):

- "I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at NVIDIA, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life. I created problems for the Board that I deeply regret."
- He said he is committed to cooperating with the board's independent investigation.
- "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO."

He had public backing. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1035420/jensen-huang-nvidia-return-as-ceo-statement) reports more than 193,000 people, including Elon Musk and Epic's Tim Sweeney, signed a letter to "bring Jensen back," and President Trump said a return "certainly isn't a bad idea."

## What the board said

Nvidia chairman Harvey C. Jones opened Tuesday's earnings call with a short statement ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-says-it-doesnt-plan-to-reappoint-jensen-huang-as-ceo/), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-national-security-chips-3026bcbe04ab57b844884382e583422a)): "The NVIDIA board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO." He added that Nvidia has hired the search firm Heidrick & Struggles to find a permanent CEO, that the board created a new National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, and that the company would not take questions about Huang's resignation or the investigation.

## The numbers

Under interim CEO Colette Kress, Nvidia's [third-quarter results](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-third-quarter-fiscal-2027) came in well above expectations:

- **Revenue:** $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago and 20% from last quarter. Analysts expected about $109.1 billion, per LSEG data cited by [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q3-2027.html).
- **Data center revenue:** $107.6 billion, up 110%.
- **Outlook:** about $130 billion in revenue next quarter.
- Nvidia says its new Vera Rubin NVL72 systems have started shipping.

"Our momentum is real," Kress said in the release.

## Why it matters to you

Nvidia's chips power most of the AI tools people use every day, and its stock is one of the largest holdings in S&P 500 index funds, so it sits in a lot of 401(k)s. Tuesday showed both sides of the story: the business is still growing fast without its founder, while the leadership question and the investigation are not settled.
