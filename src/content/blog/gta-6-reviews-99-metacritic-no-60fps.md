If you preordered Grand Theft Auto VI, the critics have spoken, and they really, really liked it.

## What happened

Rockstar's review embargo for **GTA VI** lifted at **6 a.m. ET on Wednesday, November 18, 2026**. According to [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-review-scores-99-metacritic-rockstar-grand-theft-auto-vi-2000757999), the first **145 reviews** added up to a **Metacritic score of 99 out of 100**, with no review lower than a 9 out of 10 at the time of writing.

That ties the record. The only other game ever to score 99 on Metacritic is the original **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time** from 1998, which has held the top spot since the site launched in 2001, as [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/gta-6-opens-to-roaring-critical-acclaim-matching-zeldas-historic-score/) notes. For comparison, GTA IV scored 98 and GTA V scored 97. On OpenCritic, 100 percent of critics recommend the game.

IGN, Game Informer and others gave it perfect scores. Both outlets point out the number can still move as more reviews are added in the coming days.

## The catch for console players: 30fps, permanently

Rockstar had already said GTA VI targets **30 frames per second** on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. Now, as [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/rockstar-confirms-gta-6-will-never-get-a-60fps-mode-on-ps5-and-xbox-series-x-s/) reports citing IGN, Rockstar has confirmed **no 60fps performance mode is coming**, not even on PS5 Pro. The reason given is the CPU: the Pro's upgraded graphics chip helps image quality, but its processor is only modestly faster, and Rockstar's huge, crowded open world leans heavily on the CPU.

Early performance reports say the base PS5 mostly holds 30fps but can dip into the mid-20s in demanding scenes, while the PS5 Pro holds steadier, per Digital Trends.

## When you can play

GTA VI is already live in **New Zealand and Australia** thanks to time zones, and it rolls out worldwide on **November 19** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One owners can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming, as we [covered last week](https://justincredibletech.com/blog/gta-6-xbox-one-cloud-30fps/). Rockstar hasn't announced a PC release date.

## Why it matters

GTA VI is the most anticipated game in years, coming 13 years after GTA V. A record-tying score makes it a likely holiday blockbuster, but the 30fps cap will be a sticking point for players used to 60fps modes on current consoles.
