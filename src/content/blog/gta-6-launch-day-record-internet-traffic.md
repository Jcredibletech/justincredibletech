*Update to our November 18 story on GTA 6's 99 Metacritic score. The game is now out.*

After a 13-year wait, **Grand Theft Auto VI** is finally in players' hands, and the internet felt it.

## What happened

Rockstar Games released GTA VI on **Thursday, November 19, 2026**, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The rollout started at midnight in New Zealand and moved across time zones, [Reuters reported](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/gamers-get-ready-to-enter-vice-city-as-grand-theft-auto-vi-begins-globalrollout-5035485). In the U.S., the game unlocked at midnight Eastern (9 p.m. Pacific on Wednesday), according to [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-release-time-pdt-edt/).

The analytics firm **Sensor Tower** estimated that more than **17 million copies** had sold for more than **$1.65 billion** so far, Reuters reported. That's an outside estimate, not a Rockstar or Take-Two figure.

## Record traffic

The download is huge: up to about **212 GB** depending on the console, [Computer Weekly reported](https://www.computerweekly.com/news/366654630/Grand-Theft-Auto-VI-launch-hammers-global-networks). Computer Weekly also reported it is the first AAA game to launch digital-only. Preloading opened November 10 to spread out the load, but networks still set records:

- Internet exchange operator **DE-CIX** said global traffic across its sites peaked at **33.03 terabits per second**, a new record.
- DE-CIX's **Dallas** exchange hit **2.87 Tbps** on November 18, another peak.
- UK providers **Virgin Media O2** and **CityFibre** also reported all-time traffic highs.

Even so, the London Internet Exchange's CEO said the launch was "a marked improvement" over 2023's Call of Duty launch, which caused widespread congestion.

## A rocky night for PlayStation

Hours before launch, the **PlayStation Network** had a major outage. Players reported trouble signing in and launching games, and the status page showed the issue resolved by **2:30 p.m. ET Wednesday**, [Axios reported](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/18/gta-6-playstation-psn-servers). Xbox players reported no issues that day.

## Why it matters

GTA VI is shaping up as one of the biggest entertainment launches ever, and it's a real-world stress test of how the internet handles a single huge download. If your download crawled last night, you weren't alone. The reviews still hold: Reuters notes the game sits at **99 on Metacritic**, tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
