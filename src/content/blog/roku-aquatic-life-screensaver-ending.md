Roku is draining the fish tank.

If you've set your Roku to the **Aquatic Life** screensaver, the one with the coral, bubbles and lazy tropical fish, you may have seen a new message pop up over it. We caught it on our own TV (that's our photo up top). It reads:

> "Starting Dec 1, 2026, the Aquatic Life screensaver will no longer be available, and your screensaver will switch to Roku City. Prefer something different? Browse the Theme Store in Settings > Theme > Screensaver."

[Cord Cutters News](https://cordcuttersnews.com/roku-is-removing-a-popular-feature-next-month/) reported Monday that other Roku users are seeing the same notice. We haven't found a separate press release or blog post from Roku explaining why, so for now the on-screen notice is the official word.

## Fans are not taking it well

On X, the reaction has been loud. People are begging @Roku to keep it, calling Roku City "too chaotic," and pushing hashtags like #SaveRokuFish. One person said the tank has been "enormously calming" for his wife, who is bedridden. A screensaver isn't supposed to be this emotional, but here we are.

## Where the fish came from

Aquatic Life has deep roots. It's Roku's version of **SereneScreen Marine Aquarium**, the realistic aquarium screensaver artist Jim Sachs released for PCs in 2000, according to [How-To Geek](https://www.howtogeek.com/forget-roku-city-i-use-this-classic-aquarium-screensaver-instead/). If you had a Windows PC in the early 2000s, there's a good chance you watched those fish. [SereneScreen says](https://serenescreen.com/about.html) Roku made it the default screensaver on its 4K streaming devices in December 2017. Roku's [support page](https://support.roku.com/article/change-the-screensaver) still lists it as a free screensaver for 4K Roku devices.

## The Roku City angle

The replacement, **Roku City**, is the animated cityscape Roku introduced in 2017. Cord Cutters News notes it has grown into a promo stage over the years. Users have spotted ads in it, from a Barbie billboard to McDonald's golden arches, and Roku has hosted "Live from Roku City" events with artists like Demi Lovato and Luke Bryan. Aquatic Life, by comparison, never showed ads beyond a Roku logo carved into a rock. Roku hasn't said that's the reason for the change, but it's hard not to notice.

## Do screensavers still matter?

Roku's own support page says screensavers were originally designed to prevent burn-in on older TVs and are now used mostly for entertainment. That said, if you own an OLED, moving images beat a frozen menu any day.

## How to switch before Dec. 1

1. Press **Home** on your Roku remote.
2. Go to **Settings > Theme > Screensavers**.
3. Pick an installed option or download a new one, then select **Set as screensaver**.

Cord Cutters News says other screensavers, like Jungle, Nautical and Star Trek: The Original Series, aren't showing the same notice. If you just want the fish back, SereneScreen still sells Marine Aquarium for Windows and Mac.
