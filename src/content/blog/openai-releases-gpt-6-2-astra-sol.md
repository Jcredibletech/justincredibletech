If you use ChatGPT for work, the model behind it just changed again. OpenAI released two updated models this week, and its main pitch isn't just "smarter" but "cheaper to get the same job done."

## What happened

On **Tuesday, November 17, 2026**, OpenAI [introduced](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol) **GPT-6.2 Astra**, which it calls its new frontier model for "your hardest projects," and **GPT-6.2 Sol**, an efficient model for everyday tasks. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1035483/openai-releases-its-updated-gpt-6-2-astra-model) and [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-wednesday-november-18-2026/) also reported the launch. Both models are available in ChatGPT Work, Codex and OpenAI's API for eligible users.

## What OpenAI says is new

According to OpenAI's announcement:

- **Work documents:** Astra's slides, spreadsheets and documents "read less like AI" and follow the user's templates and field conventions more closely.
- **Coding:** cleaner code and better-looking front ends, especially for websites, games and 3D design.
- **Efficiency:** both models are said to finish demanding tasks with fewer output tokens, so users get more done within plan limits.
- **Benchmarks:** on Agents' Last Exam, a test of multistep professional work, OpenAI says Astra scored **5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra** at less than a third of the cost. It also claims new highs on two science benchmarks.
- **Sol:** OpenAI says Sol came within **0.5 points** of Astra on the FrontierCode 2.0 coding test at about one-fifth the cost.

These benchmark results come from OpenAI; independent testing will take time.

## Pricing

In the API, Astra costs **$8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens**, about 20% less than GPT-6 Astra, OpenAI says. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's prices.

## Safety notes

OpenAI's [GPT-6.2 system card](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-2-astra-sol), also highlighted by The Neuron, says Astra is the company's first externally released model to meet its "High" threshold for AI self-improvement, which OpenAI describes as an early milestone for preparing safeguards, not a sign of fully automated self-improvement. Both models meet OpenAI's "Critical" threshold for cybersecurity capabilities, so the company says it tightened default limits on higher-risk cybersecurity help. That lands the same week a critical Java bug was found with help from an AI coding tool.

## Why it matters to you

The AI race is shifting from raw capability to cost per task. Cheaper, more efficient models mean more of these tools get built into the apps and services you already use, and price competition between OpenAI, Anthropic and Google is heating up as Anthropic heads into its IPO.
