If you use ChatGPT — or you just watch AI stocks — this is the money number of the day. **Bloomberg** reported Friday that **OpenAI has closed about $11 billion** in new funding at a **$1.25 trillion** valuation, according to people familiar with the matter. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/), [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/openai-secures-11-billion-funding-at-125-trillion-valuation-bloomberg-news-reports-5009182), and others amplified the same Bloomberg sourcing.

## What closed — and what didn't

That **$11 billion** is the **first portion** of a larger round. Bloomberg says the round **hasn't fully closed** yet and could still grow to about **$20 billion** in the coming weeks. Back in September, the same outlet said OpenAI had been aiming for roughly **$30 billion** at a **$1.4 trillion** valuation. Today's figures land well short of that ask.

For context: OpenAI's **March** raise was reported at about **$122 billion** at an **$852 billion** valuation. Friday's close marks another step up in paper value — and a clearer signal that private-market appetite for mega AI rounds is cooling after this week's public-market sell-off.

## Why it matters to you

Viewer stakes are simple:

- **ChatGPT users:** more cash for compute, products, and data-center buildouts — the same infrastructure fight voters just punished at the ballot box in many places.
- **Investors / 401(k) watchers:** OpenAI is still private, but the valuation print moves the whole AI trade. Anthropic IPO buyers, Bloomberg also reported Friday, are already talking numbers as low as about **$1.1 trillion**, down from earlier stretch talk near **$2 trillion**.
- **Workers in AI-adjacent jobs:** when fundraising targets shrink, hiring, infra deals, and partner commitments often follow.

## The fine print

OpenAI has not publicly confirmed the Bloomberg figures as of this writing; TechCrunch said it reached out for comment. Treat the numbers as **reported**, not a company press release. The round may still expand. And a $1.25 trillion private mark is not the same thing as a public IPO price — OpenAI delayed going public this year, with expectations now leaning toward **sometime next year**.

So: eleven billion is in. The valuation sticker says one-point-two-five trillion. The market around AI just got a cold splash of water. **Would you buy in at that price?**
