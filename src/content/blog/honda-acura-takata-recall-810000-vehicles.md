A day after federal regulators posted Toyota's latest Takata recall, Honda is pulling in more than 800,000 older cars over Takata airbag parts, this time a type that wasn't part of the earlier recalls.

## What happened

On **Friday, November 20, 2026**, **American Honda** [announced](https://hondanews.com/en-US/honda-corporate/releases/release-b26ae02ff5dca50cb32a1f47831d8dd1-statement-by-american-honda-regarding-takata-desiccated-airbag-inflator-recall) it will recall **810,592 vehicles** in the U.S. to replace Takata front driver and/or passenger airbag inflators. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/honda-to-recall-more-than-800000-us-vehicles-over-takata-air-bag-rupture-risk-5042550) reported the recall the same day, citing the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

The recalled models:

- **2013 Honda Accord**
- **2012-2013 Honda Civic**
- **2012-2013 Honda CR-V**
- **2013 Acura ILX**
- **2013 Acura RDX**

Only vehicles **originally sold or ever registered** in these hot, humid places are included: **Alabama, California, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Louisiana, Mississippi, South Carolina, Texas, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, the Northern Mariana Islands (Saipan), Guam and American Samoa.**

## What's different about these airbags

The massive Takata recalls of the last decade involved inflators **without** a drying agent, called a desiccant, which could degrade and rupture, spraying metal fragments. These newly recalled inflators **do** contain a desiccant and weren't part of the earlier recalls. Honda says its long-term monitoring found "potential propellent degradation in certain inflators within specific geographic regions," so it's acting as a precaution.

Honda says **no ruptures, injuries or deaths** have been reported from these specific inflators.

## What owners should do

- **Check your VIN now** at [recalls.honda.com](https://www.recalls.honda.com) or [recalls.acura.com](https://www.recalls.acura.com), or call (888) 234-2138.
- **Mailed notices start in December 2026.**
- The repair is **free** at an authorized Honda or Acura dealer. Honda is urging owners to get it done as soon as they're notified.

If you bought a used 2012 or 2013 Honda or Acura, it's worth checking even if you live elsewhere, since a car that was *ever* registered in one of those states counts.

## Why it matters

This is the second big Takata-related recall to surface this week. On November 19 we covered [Toyota's recall of about 314,000 vehicles](/blog/toyota-takata-airbag-recall-314000-vehicles/), and together the two total roughly 1.1 million vehicles. It's a sign that Takata inflators outside the original recalls are getting a second look as these cars age in heat and humidity.
