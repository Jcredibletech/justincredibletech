If your iPhone or Mac suddenly signed you out of iCloud on Tuesday, or Apple Pay failed at checkout, it wasn't just you.

## What happened

On **Tuesday, November 17, 2026**, a wide range of Apple's online services ran into trouble. According to [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2319504/icloud-and-other-apple-services-are-down-in-an-apparent-outage/), a handful of Downdetector reports started around **11:30 a.m. ET** (10:30 a.m. CT), then jumped into the **thousands** around 12:29 p.m. ET.

People reported problems with:

- **iCloud** sign-in, including unexpected sign-outs on iPhone and Mac followed by "verification failed" errors ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/17/icloud-app-store-and-other-apple-services-hit-by-ongoing-outage/))
- **Apple Pay** and Apple Card
- The **App Store** and **Apple Music**
- **Device activation**, which meant some new devices couldn't be set up ([The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/tech/apple-down-not-working-pay-icloud-music-app-store-iphone-status-b3086668.html))

It reached Apple's own stores, too. A customer told [NBC10 Philadelphia](https://www.nbcphiladelphia.com/news/tech/apple-reports-outages-for-multiple-services/4498868/) that an employee at the Walnut Street Apple Store said the store couldn't process transactions because of the outage.

## What Apple says

Apple hasn't announced a cause. Its [System Status page](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/) listed **iCloud Account & Sign In** as "slow or unavailable" and said AppleCare purchases on devices were temporarily unavailable. Engadget reported the page also showed outages for the App Store and Apple Music. As of about 1 p.m. CT, iCloud sign-in was still flagged, along with Apple Business, Apple School Manager and Schoolwork.

There are signs of recovery. [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/news/live/apple-outage-november-17-2026) reported that Apple Pay and Apple Card problem reports dropped steeply, and that Apple's developer status page marked In-App Purchases as resolved, though App Store Connect was still down for developers.

## Why it matters

So much of daily life now runs through one account: your photos, passwords, payments and the apps on your phone. When that sign-in breaks, everything tied to it can stall at once. This also follows a rough stretch for big tech services, including [last week's Microsoft 365 outage](https://justincredibletech.com/blog/microsoft-365-outlook-outage/) and [Monday's Chase online banking problems](https://justincredibletech.com/blog/chase-software-bug-online-banking-outage/). The Independent notes the run of outages has sparked theories that they're connected, but the official causes given for the others so far have been ordinary technical faults. This story is still developing, and we'll update it if Apple explains what went wrong.
