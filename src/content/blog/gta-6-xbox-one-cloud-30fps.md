Still have the Xbox One you bought back in 2013? It just got a second life. Nine days before launch, Rockstar Games and Microsoft confirmed that **Grand Theft Auto VI** will be playable on Xbox One on day one, not natively, but streamed from Microsoft's servers through Xbox Cloud Gaming. "GTA 6," "GTA VI," "Rockstar," "Xbox One," "PS5 Pro" and "30fps" were all trending on X in the U.S. this afternoon.

## What happened

- **Xbox One gets it through the cloud.** Grand Theft Auto VI will stream through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles starting at 1 a.m. ET on November 19. The streamed version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/)). You still have to buy the game, and Rockstar warns that connection speed can affect performance in ways local play wouldn't.
- **Consoles only, for now.** The Verge reports that the cloud version is limited to Xbox consoles, not PCs, phones or TVs, and Xbox CEO Asha Sharma called the deal "the first step in our new 10-year strategic partnership with Take-Two" ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/1030714/gta-vi-xbox-cloud-gaming-support)). GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 are headed to Xbox Cloud Gaming in the coming months.
- **30fps across the board.** In its November 10 Newswire post, Rockstar said the game targets 30 frames per second on current consoles "while pushing both platforms to their absolute limits." There's no mention of a 60fps mode. PS5 Pro owners get PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) upscaling and ray-traced shadows that Rockstar says are only on PS5 Pro ([Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-now-available-ps5-pro-support-confirmed), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2312120/gta-6-is-targeting-30-fps-and-has-ray-tracing-support-on-ps5-pro/)).
- **Pre-loads are live.** Digital pre-orders can download now, and the game needs up to 212 GB of space depending on your console. Code-in-box physical copies start hitting stores November 12 ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/gta-6-30fps-ps5-pro-xbox-cloud/)).

## Why it matters to you

If you were about to drop hundreds of dollars on a new console just for GTA 6, you now have a cheaper option, as long as your internet can handle streaming. Starting November 11, Microsoft says players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime without a Game Pass subscription. And if you already own a PS5 Pro hoping for 60fps, Rockstar's answer for now is sharper visuals and better shadows, not more frames.

## My take

This is competition doing its job. Microsoft found a way to put the biggest game of the decade on a 13-year-old box, Sony is pushing its Pro hardware perks, and gamers get more ways to play without anybody in Washington having to referee it.
