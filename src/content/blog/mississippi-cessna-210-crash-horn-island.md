Six people from one Fort Myers, Florida, family died when their single-engine **Cessna 210** crashed into the Gulf of Mexico off the Mississippi coast on **Tuesday afternoon, November 24**. The plane went down between **Pascagoula and Horn Island**, part of Gulf Islands National Seashore, about 2 miles northeast of the island, according to the Coast Guard and the Jackson County coroner.

## What happened

The plane left **New Orleans Lakefront Airport** at 2:02 p.m. and was headed to **Page Field** in Fort Myers, the [Biloxi Sun Herald](https://www.sunherald.com/news/local/counties/jackson-county/article318246221.html) reports. Air traffic controllers in Mobile, Alabama, lost contact at about **3:45 p.m.**, according to the Coast Guard. The FAA said the plane was **descending at about 10,000 feet per minute** when contact was lost, [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/small-plane-crash-off-mississippi-6-dead-2-children/) reports, citing the Coast Guard via WLOX-TV.

Searchers first found three life jackets, a life raft and part of the cabin floating on the water. After about four hours, divers from the **Mississippi Department of Marine Resources** found all six people inside the fuselage, the Coast Guard said.

"While this is not the outcome any of us had hoped for, we are grateful for the strong interagency coordination that enabled us to swiftly locate those aboard the aircraft and provide closure to their families," said Cmdr. Trevor Blount, the Coast Guard's search-and-rescue mission coordinator for Sector Mobile, as quoted by CBS and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c6z74l1017zpo).

## Who was aboard

On Wednesday, Jackson County Coroner **Bruce Lynd Jr.** identified the victims as members of one extended family: the pilot, **Brent Stokes, 50**; his son, 16; his sister and her husband, both 43; and their two sons, 12 and 10, according to the Sun Herald. The Coast Guard had first described the group as four adults and two boys ages 8 and 10. The coroner's identifications came later. Local outlets including [NOLA.com](https://www.nola.com/news/horn-island-plane-crash-victims/article_1997cbff-b356-4b67-a294-fc2b11303a8c.html) report the family was returning from a fishing trip in Louisiana.

## What's next

The **National Transportation Safety Board** is leading the investigation with the FAA. No cause has been announced. The NTSB usually releases a preliminary report within a few weeks of a crash, and a final probable-cause report often takes a year or more. The weather, the aircraft's maintenance history and the rapid descent the FAA described will all be part of that review.

The crash comes during a busy Thanksgiving travel week and weeks after [a Learjet 60 crashed into a Florida neighborhood, killing nine](/blog/learjet-60-crash-greenacres-florida/). Small general-aviation planes like the Cessna 210 are a different category from airline flights, which also had a rough week with [Delta's weight-and-balance system outage](/blog/delta-outage-weight-balance-system-flight-delays/).
