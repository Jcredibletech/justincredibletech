Days after the U.S. officially lost its measles elimination status, Virginia is reporting the kind of outcome public health officials have been warning about.

## What happened

On **Friday, November 20, 2026**, the **Virginia Department of Health (VDH)** [announced](https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/news/public-relations-contacts/2026-news-releases/vdh-1st-measles-death-2026/) that a **young child, between 0 and 4 years old**, who tested positive for measles has died. It's Virginia's **first measles-associated death of 2026**. To protect the family's privacy, VDH said it will not release any other details, including where the child lived.

VDH said there has been "a recent increase in measles activity in some areas of Virginia" and that its measles dashboard will be updated on **Tuesday, November 24**.

## The national picture

According to [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Health/child-measles-dies-virginia-marking-states-1st-measles/story?id=138330916):

- This is the **12th measles-associated death** reported by U.S. state health departments in 2026, with others in New York, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin.
- The CDC's latest data shows **5,838 confirmed measles cases** this year in nearly every state.
- About **96%** of cases are in people who are unvaccinated or whose vaccination status is unknown.
- About **one in six children under 5** with measles has been hospitalized.

The death comes days after the Pan American Health Organization confirmed the U.S. lost the **measles elimination status** it had held since 2000, which we covered [here](/blog/update-paho-confirms-us-loses-measles-elimination-status/).

## What health officials say

This is public health guidance from the agencies, not medical advice for any individual:

- VDH says two doses of the **MMR (measles, mumps, rubella) vaccine** are about **97% effective** at preventing measles; the CDC figures cited by ABC News say one dose is about 93% effective.
- The CDC recommends the first dose at **12 to 15 months** and the second at **4 to 6 years**.
- VDH says children under 5, pregnant people and people with weakened immune systems face the highest risk of complications, which can include pneumonia and brain inflammation.
- Anyone who develops a **fever and rash**, especially after a possible exposure, should **call ahead** before going to a clinic or hospital so staff can avoid exposing others, VDH says. Virginians can check their vaccine records through VDH's online record request portal or their doctor.

## Why it matters

Measles is one of the most contagious diseases known, and young children carry the highest risk. The CDC data cited by ABC News also shows kindergarten MMR coverage slipping to **92.5%** in the 2024-2025 school year, down from **95.2%** in 2019-2020, before the COVID-19 pandemic.
