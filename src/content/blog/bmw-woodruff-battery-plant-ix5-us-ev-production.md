BMW has built cars in South Carolina for three decades, but never a fully electric one. That's about to change. On Friday, November 13, BMW officially opened **Plant Woodruff**, a battery assembly plant that will supply packs for the new **BMW iX5**, the first fully electric BMW built in the United States.

## What happened

According to [BMW Group](https://newsroom.bmwgroup.com/americas/bmw-group-opens-high-voltage-battery-assembly-plant-in-woodruff-and-marks-the-start-of-fully-electric-vehicle-production-in-the-us/), the plant has started **series production of sixth-generation (Gen6) high-voltage batteries**. Finished packs travel just **15 miles** to BMW's giant Plant Spartanburg, where they'll go into the iX5. [BMWBLOG reports](https://www.bmwblog.com/2026/11/13/bmw-woodruff-battery-plant-ix5/) iX5 production there is due to start before the end of the year.

A few key facts:

- **Cost:** about **$700 million**, part of a $1.7 billion package BMW announced in 2022 that also included $1 billion to prepare Spartanburg for EVs.
- **Timeline:** groundbreaking in June 2023, test packs in summer 2025, series production now.
- **People and machines:** more than **300 employees** and about **250 robots**. BMW says it uses in-house AI tools on production data and trained staff in a virtual-reality copy of the factory.
- **Network:** Woodruff is the fourth of five Gen6 battery plants to start production, after sites in Hungary, Germany and China. A plant in Mexico follows in 2027.

## About the iX5 and its battery

Per BMWBLOG, the U.S.-spec **iX5 60 xDrive** uses a **144-kWh** pack, has a preliminary BMW range estimate of **435 miles**, and starts at **$79,800** plus a $1,450 destination charge. It goes on sale in the first quarter of 2027. Those range and pricing figures are BMW's preliminary numbers, not final EPA ratings.

One detail to know: Woodruff **assembles packs but doesn't make the battery cells**. BMW buys the cells from suppliers. BMWBLOG reports AESC is supplying them for now after pausing its own South Carolina cell plant in 2025, with LG Energy Solution's Arizona plant expected to start deliveries in the late 2020s.

BMW claims its Gen6 round cells have more than **20% higher energy density** than its previous generation, with up to **30% faster charging** and **30% more range**.

## Why it matters

Building batteries close to the assembly line cuts shipping and ties BMW's EV plans more tightly to the U.S. BMW says it will assemble at least **six fully electric models** at Spartanburg by 2030. It also comes as the U.S. EV market is cooling after federal tax credits ended, so this is a long-term bet that demand will be there when these models arrive.
