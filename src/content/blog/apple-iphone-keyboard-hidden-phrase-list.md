Ever swipe-typed a phrase on your iPhone and watched it turn into something weird? It might not be you. A researcher says the iOS keyboard carries an undisclosed list of roughly **100,000 words and phrases** it will quietly refuse to suggest.

## What was found

- Researcher **Derik Kauffman** told **Reuters** he found a multilingual "gray list" stored deep inside iOS, using OpenAI's Codex and Anthropic's Claude Code to dig it out. He puts a conservative count at about 100,000 entries, not counting Chinese characters ([Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusiveapples-predictive-text-restrictions-include-dictator-trump-and-sleepy-joe-researcher-finds-5007215)).
- It's **not a ban**. You can still type anything letter by letter. But phrases on the list won't show up as predictive text, and swipe typing usually swaps in a different word. Reuters tested a sample on an iPhone: "Sleepy Joe" came out as "Sleepy hope," and "United Ireland" as "United Iceland."
- Most entries are slurs, insults, or sex and drug terms. But politics is in there too. **The Verge** lists "dictator Trump," "sleepy Joe," "illegal immigrant," "covid was a hoax" and "left wing conspiracy" ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028663/apple-ios-keyboard-blocked-words-autocomplete)). Kauffman's site, [Swipeblocked](https://swipeblocked.com/), which decodes the list from iOS 27.0's keyboard files, also shows "make America great again" as blocked from suggestions (it can still be swiped).
- Some pairs are lopsided: "bad girl" is blocked but "bad boy" isn't, per The Verge. Even "Santa isn't real" and "iMessage isn't working" made the list ([AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/11/06/swipeblocked-catalogs-the-phrases-apples-keyboard-wont-suggest)).
- Apple didn't comment on the list's size or contents. It told Reuters the keyboard is designed to avoid suggestions that might be inappropriate or offensive, and that users can always type what they want or override suggestions ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/06/struggling-to-spell-male-chauvinist-pig-apple-grey-list-means-your-iphone-wont-help-you)).

## Why it matters

Hundreds of millions of people type on that keyboard every day, and predictive text nudges what we write. Nobody outside Apple knew which phrases were being filtered or why until a researcher reverse-engineered it.

## My take

Keeping slurs out of autocomplete is fair enough. But political phrases are a different thing. When a trillion-dollar company quietly decides that "make America great again" or "illegal immigrant" shouldn't be suggested, that's a judgment call about speech made with zero transparency. To be fair, "dictator Trump" and "sleepy Joe" are on there too, so it's not one-sided. I just think Apple should publish the list and give users a switch to turn the political filtering off. Let adults pick their own words.
