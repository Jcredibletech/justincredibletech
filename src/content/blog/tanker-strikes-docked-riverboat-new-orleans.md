A tanker struck a docked river cruise boat on the Mississippi River in New Orleans around **12:30 p.m. Central time on Friday, November 13, 2026**, the Port of New Orleans said, setting off a large rescue effort along the riverfront.

The Port said the tanker **Atlantic Asphalt** collided with American Cruise Lines' **American Heritage** while the paddlewheel riverboat was docked outside 1350 Port of New Orleans Place, near mile marker 96. The boat had arrived about two hours earlier and was beginning to let passengers off. About **129 passengers**, plus crew members, were aboard, according to the Port, a figure also reported by the [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/new-orleans-tanker-river-cruise-rescue-passengers-757bed7a70ceb72f0d7140143fe2a764), [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/vessel-collision-port-new-orleans-emergency-crews-responding/story?id=138119847) and [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/tanker-slams-docked-new-orleans-river-cruiser-carrying-129-passengers-sparking-rescue).

AP and local outlets described photographs and video showing the riverboat listing and left **partially submerged**. The New Orleans Fire Department received an alert that a vessel was taking on water at 12:30 p.m., [The Times-Picayune / The Advocate](https://www.nola.com/news/live-updates-cruise-ship-riverboat-crash/article_00272945-1dbc-42dc-9b6a-e9c6587d9e05.html) reported. Officials have not released a cause.

## Rescue and the latest status

Port NOLA Harbor Police, the New Orleans Fire Department, the New Orleans Police Department, the U.S. Coast Guard and emergency medical crews were on scene. Passengers were being brought into the Port of New Orleans Administration Building for triage, the Port said, calling the situation "an active and evolving incident" and saying the number of injuries had not been confirmed.

As of mid-afternoon Central time, [WDSU](https://www.wdsu.com/article/mississippi-river-boat-crash-new-orleans/74426309) reported that city officials said more than a dozen people had been taken to area hospitals and that at least 12 people were unaccounted for. Fox News reported that Acadian Ambulance said it had transported eight patients. Those figures have not been issued as a final official count.

American Cruise Lines said it was coordinating with the Coast Guard and local authorities, adding: "Guests and crew were evacuated, and we are deeply concerned for everyone involved."

The Coast Guard said the Mississippi River was **closed to all vessel traffic between mile markers 93 and 96** while responders assessed the situation. The New Orleans Regional Transit Authority temporarily suspended ferry service across the river as a precaution, according to WDSU and The Times-Picayune / The Advocate. Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry said he was heading to the city. Lt. Gov. Billy Nungesser said he was working to secure hotel rooms for about 85 passengers, WDSU reported.

## Why it matters

The closure covers a busy stretch of the lower Mississippi at the Port of New Orleans, a major U.S. cargo and cruise gateway. Until that roughly three-mile segment reopens, commercial vessel traffic there is halted, and local ferry riders have been told to use other routes. Officials have not released details on the tanker's cargo or reported a spill; this account does not speculate about environmental impact. The water temperature was about **66 degrees Fahrenheit**, according to figures cited by AP, Fox News and WDSU.
