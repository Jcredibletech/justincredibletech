Robotaxis can drive you across town, but for now Waymo won't take you on the freeway anywhere in the country.

## What happened

Waymo said this week that it has **temporarily restricted driverless freeway operations in all of its markets**, after California's Department of Motor Vehicles banned its robotaxis from state freeways, according to the [AP](https://apnews.com/article/waymo-driverless-vehicle-california-freeway-ban-37bcd5371a4699a1850b7666b271946b), the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmy86pp336qlo) and [AFP](https://www.afp.com/en/waymo-pulls-self-driving-cars-us-freeways-after-california-ban).

Waymo had offered freeway rides in the **San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, Phoenix, Miami and Austin**. Its service on regular city streets continues in all of its cities.

## Why California acted

The DMV's restriction took effect **November 11**. The agency said Waymo's driverless freeway operations "pose an imminent hazard to public safety due to recurring incursions by its vehicles into active construction zones where construction workers, vehicles and equipment are present."

[NBC Los Angeles](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/california-news/dmv-ban-waymo-california-freeways/3971907/) reported the DMV cited two Bay Area incidents:

- **Three Waymo vehicles entered an active construction closure** on U.S. 101 near Burlingame.
- A Waymo tried to **pass California Highway Patrol cars** that were running a traffic break on **Interstate 280** in San Francisco, in video that spread widely online.

Waymo says the construction-zone encounters caused **no injuries or collisions**. The ban stays in place until Waymo shows the DMV it has fixed the problems, including evidence its cars can safely detect and respond to construction zones and emergency vehicles.

## Not the first time

This is Waymo's second freeway pause this year. After similar construction-zone incursions on May 18, it halted freeway rides and resumed them in late July, NBC Los Angeles reported. [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/transportation/6227655-waymo-freeway-rides-safety-hazards-suspension/) noted Waymo recalled nearly 4,000 robotaxis in June after identifying 13 instances of its vehicles driving into highway construction zones in Arizona and California.

## What Waymo says

"In alignment with our safety-first principles, we have also temporarily restricted freeway operations across our other operating markets to evaluate our performance," a Waymo spokesperson said. The company says it serves **over half a million trips each week** and is working with the DMV and other state officials to resume freeway service.

## Why it matters to you

Riders who relied on freeway trips may see longer, slower routes on surface streets. More broadly, it's a test of how regulators handle driverless cars at highway speeds, as Waymo and Tesla both race to grow their robotaxi fleets.
