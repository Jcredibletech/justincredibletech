The Food and Drug Administration, which oversees everything from prescription drugs to vapes to the food supply, still has no confirmed leader, and its nominee is out.

## What happened

Late on **Tuesday, November 17, 2026**, the White House withdrew **Dr. Heidi Overton's** nomination to be FDA commissioner, [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/17/heidi-overton-fda-nomination-dropped) first reported. Reuters, as published by [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/trump-withdraws-heidi-overton-as-nominee-to-lead-fda-sources-say.html), confirmed it with three people familiar with the matter, and the [AP](https://apnews.com/article/fda-overton-trump-senate-kennedy-cassidy-15d9c3978ed4f77657900cc2c327d375) and [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/18/nx-s1-6042516/white-house-withdraws-nominee-for-fda-commissioner) also reported the withdrawal.

"FDA nominee Heidi Overton lacks the support on the Hill to move forward with her confirmation," a senior administration official told Axios. The Senate Health, Education, Labor and Pensions (HELP) Committee had been set to vote on her nomination Wednesday. Republicans hold a one-vote edge on the panel, so a single GOP defection could have sunk it, Reuters reported.

## Who she is

Overton is a preventive medicine physician and deputy director of the White House Domestic Policy Council. President Trump nominated her in **August**, three months after former Commissioner **Marty Makary** resigned in May.

## Where the objections came from

Concerns came from several directions, according to Axios and Reuters:

- **Vaccines:** HELP Chairman **Bill Cassidy (R-La.)**, a physician, said he had "strong concerns" about her role in the administration's push to change childhood vaccine recommendations, which he called "almost disqualifying." Overton appeared with Trump in August when he signed an executive order on the childhood vaccine schedule.
- **Abortion pill:** some anti-abortion advocates questioned her commitment to tightening rules on **mifepristone**, while Democrats criticized her opposition to the Biden-era loosening of those rules. The FDA says its safety review of the drug will conclude by March 2027.
- **MAHA movement:** some supporters of HHS Secretary **Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s** "Make America Healthy Again" agenda criticized what they saw as her ties to corporate interests.
- **Vapes:** at her September hearing she defended the FDA's May authorization of fruit-flavored vapes, which drew criticism from an advocacy group.

## What the White House says

"We need to go in a new direction at FDA, and everybody fully supports Secretary Kennedy's vision to Make America Healthy Again," the senior administration official told Axios.

## What happens next

The administration needs a new nominee. Two people told Reuters the White House is weighing **Sen. Roger Marshall (R-Kan.)**, a former obstetrician. In the meantime, **Kyle Diamantas** remains acting commissioner. Reuters reports the agency has lost more than 4,500 staff over the past year.

## Why it matters to you

The FDA commissioner shapes decisions on drug approvals, vaccine guidance, food safety and vaping rules that touch nearly every household.
