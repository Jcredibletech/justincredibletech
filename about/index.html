An open letter to our readers

Why JustinCredibleTech exists, and what you can expect from it.

Dear reader,

Thanks for stopping by. If you found your way here, there’s a good chance you’re the kind of person who sees a headline like “wait, what just shipped?” and wants the rest of the story. Same here. That’s exactly why I built this place.

You may already know me as@JCredibleTech. Short posts are great for breaking news, but some stories need room to breathe: the context, the timeline, and the honest answer to “why should I care?” This site is where I give them that room.

Here’s what I chase every day: consumer tech drops and the specs that actually matter, AI that changes how we work and play, cars and EVs (Tesla and everyone racing it), spaceflight and Starlink, science and medical breakthroughs, and the big non-political news that touches all of it. If it’s new, real, and worth your time, I want to explain it clearly.

My promise to you is simple. I’ll keep it accurate, keep it fun, and keep politics out of it. If something is a rumor, I’ll call it a rumor. If a company publishes a date and a price, you’ll get the date and the price, not hype dressed up as news.

If you want the quick hits, follow along on@JCredibleTech. If you want the full story, start with the latest posts, or grab the RSS feed and read on your own schedule.

However you got here, I’m genuinely glad you did. Thanks for reading, and thanks for being curious.

-Justin
