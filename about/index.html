About JustinCredibleTech

Long-form companion to @JCredibleTech, for when a post needs room to breathe.

Hey — I’m Justin McBroom. Online you’ll usually see me as@JCredibleTech. Short posts are great for the “wait, what just shipped?” moments. This site is for the follow-up: context, timelines, and the “why should I care?” without the fluff.

What you’ll find here

JustinCredibleTech covers the beat I already chase in socials — consumer tech drops, spaceflight milestones, and science that actually touches technology. Think new phones and foldables, lunar AI models, Starlink coverage leaps, EV and robotaxi reality checks, and the companies that keep showing up in that orbit: Tesla, SpaceX, xAI, Starlink, and the broader EV / space / AI stack.

  • Consumer tech — launches, specs that matter, and what “available Friday” really means.
  • Space — missions, maps, and orbital internet that changes who gets a signal.
  • Science × tech — open models, ice prospectivity, sensors, and tools researchers can actually download.
  • Mobility & energy — EVs, autonomy, and the infrastructure that makes the demos stick.

How I write

Voice check: lighthearted, informative, and fun. I aim for accurate facts, clear structure, and zero politics. If a rumor isn’t confirmed, I’ll say so. If Apple, NASA, or SpaceX published a date and a price, you’ll get those — not vibes dressed up as news.

Reading experience matters too. Articles here are set for long-form: comfortable line length, readable type, and the basics done right — RSS, sitemap, and sensible SEO titles so you can subscribe or share without fighting the page.

Stay in the loop

Short takes land on@JCredibleTech. Longer reads live here. If you want both, follow there and bookmark this site — or start with thelatest posts and grab theRSS feed for your own schedule.
