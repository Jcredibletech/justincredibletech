Apple’s iPhone Duo is real — foldable, $1,999, ships October 23

Apple unveiled its first foldable iPhone on September 9, 2026. Here’s the price, ship date, and how it sits next to iPhone 18 Pro.

  • Apple
  • iPhone
  • foldable
  • consumer tech

Apple finally said the quiet part out loud: the foldable iPhone is not a rumor cycle anymore. On September 9, 2026, Apple unveiled iPhone Duo — its first foldable smartphone — and put real dates and a real starting price on the table. If you’ve been waiting for the company that popularized the modern smartphone to enter the book-style foldable game, this is the week that wait ended.

The short version

  • Announced: September 9, 2026
  • Starts at: $1,999 (U.S.) for 256GB
  • Pre-orders: Friday, October 16, 2026 at 5:00 a.m. PT
  • Availability / shipping: Friday, October 23, 2026 (first wave; more regions October 30)
  • Colors: star white and night sky
  • Storage: 256GB, 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB (top configs climb as high as $3,199)

That $1,999 entry price is… not subtle. It’s also honest about where Apple is placing Duo: not a cheap experiment, but a flagship form factor with a flagship bill. Apple is also offering 24-month installment math (around $83.29/month at the base price) and, in the U.S., Apple Upgrade leasing options through Klarna that can start lower depending on term.

What “Duo” is trying to be

Apple’s pitch is classic Apple: make the new thing feel inevitable. Open, Duo is positioned as the thinnest iPhone with the largest display Apple has shipped — Apple says the inner canvas is about 50% larger than iPhone 18 Pro Max. Closed, there’s an outer display Apple claims covers more than 90% of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro. In other words: pocketable candy-bar when you need it, tablet-adjacent when you unfold it.

The interesting part isn’t only the hinge theater. It’s that Apple is tying the hardware to reimagined iOS layouts — poses and orientations that only make sense when a phone can be a book, a mini laptop stand, or a dual-surface camera rig. Foldables from other brands already proved the form factor can work. Duo’s job is to make it feel like an iPhone first and a foldable second.

Accessories land on the same pre-order day: an iPhone Duo Case at $79 (navy blue / sand) and an iPhone Duo Folio with Kickstand at $129 (navy blue / taupe). That’s Apple signaling this isn’t a one-SKU novelty — it’s a product line with its own ecosystem from day one.

Don’t sleep on the candy-bar Pros

Same keynote energy, different timeline: iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are the traditional path for people who want “new iPhone” without learning a hinge. They were announced alongside the foldable news on September 9. Pre-orders start September 12 at 5:00 a.m. PT, and they hit shelves Friday, September 18, 2026 — a full month before Duo ships.

Pricing for the Pros starts lower than Duo (Pro from $1,199, Pro Max from $1,299 in the U.S., with 2TB as a new top storage tier). Finishes include black, silver, glacier, and a new burgundy. If your takeaway from September is “I just want the best conventional iPhone,” Apple made that path clear and fast.

Duo is the conversation piece. 18 Pro is the volume play. Apple is running both plays on purpose.

Who Duo is actually for

Be honest with your wallet. At two thousand dollars before tax, Duo is for:

  1. Early adopters who want the first Apple foldable in the drawer next to the first iPhone SE nostalgia brick.
  2. Productivity weirdos (affectionate) who live in split views, travel docs, and “prop it open on a tray table” workflows.
  3. People who already stretch for Pro Max every year and were going to spend big anyway — Duo just redirects the spend into a new shape.

Everyone else can watch the reviews, wait for a refreshed Duo next cycle, or grab 18 Pro on September 18 and call it a day. That’s not shade — that’s how first-gen Apple hardware usually goes. The first version proves the idea. Later versions sand the edges.

The JustinCredible take

I’ve been covering foldable rumors long enough to develop a callus. Seeing Apple put October 23 and $1,999 on an official Newsroom post hits different. It means the supply chain, software, and durability story are far enough along that Apple is willing to sell it in more than 70 countries in the first wave.

Will Duo “kill” the slab iPhone? No. Slab phones still win on pocket simplicity and price. Will Duo matter? Yes — because once Apple ships a category, developers, accessory makers, and competitors all recalibrate. The foldable market just got an Apple-shaped gravity well.

If you’re pre-ordering: set the October 16 alarm, know your storage tier, and decide whether you want star white or night sky before the forums decide for you. If you’re waiting: enjoy the 18 Pro launch on the 18th, read the durability teardowns, and let the early Duo owners be the unpaid QA team. Either way, September 2026 just became a landmark month for iPhone hardware — one path folds, one path doesn’t, and both are shipping on the calendar for real.
